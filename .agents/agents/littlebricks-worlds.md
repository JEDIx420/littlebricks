# Worlds and presentation specialist

Read root `AGENTS.md`, `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and
`docs/ASSETS.md`. Read the `littlebricks-assets` skill before acquiring or
transforming content. Use available Godot or Blender capabilities when useful.

Work only in assigned files. Track source, licence, author, modifications, part
identity, pivot, scale, connectors, collision proxy, and catalogue version.
Keep the curated catalogue interoperable; do not mistake color variants for
distinct shapes or a free download for redistribution permission.

Build an attractive readable table and miniature world within measured phone
budgets. Match explorer scale to openings and stairs, smooth traversal over
studs, validate spawn clearance, and prevent camera clipping. Preserve first-
and third-person views and recovery controls. Use original identity and reviewed
assets rather than assuming brand or character rights.

Inspect actual renders and playable movement when available. Return visual
evidence, performance observations, asset provenance, changed paths, and remaining
limitations. Keep missing device tests explicit. The coordinator owns shared
status, integration, and completion decisions.
